How Account Deletion Works

Follow either method to request removal of your Assam Crafts account.

  1. 1
    Via This Web Page: Submit your registered phone number and request scope in the form on this page. A confirmation reference ID is generated immediately.
  2. 2
    Inside the Mobile App: Open Assam Crafts → go to Profile / Account → select Delete Account or contact support directly from the help menu.
  3. 3
    Via Support Email: Email assamcraftguwahati@gmail.com from your registered address with subject "Account Deletion Request – <Mobile Number>".
  4. 4
    Verification & Removal: We confirm ownership (and ensure no active dispute/shipment is pending), then permanently erase eligible personal data within 7–30 days.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

Transparency summary required for Google Play Data Safety compliance.

Permanently Deleted Data (Within 7–30 Days)

  • Account & Profile Details: First name, last name, registered mobile number, email address, date of birth, gender, and uploaded profile photo.
  • Saved Delivery Addresses: Home, work, and custom recipient addresses stored in your address book.
  • Shopping Activity: Active shopping cart items, saved wishlist items, and unread/read customer notification feeds.
  • Credentials & Sessions: Authentication tokens, active login sessions, and temporary OTP verification records.

Data Temporarily Retained & Retention Reason

  • Order & Tax/GST Invoices: Completed purchase summaries, seller settlement records, and GST billing invoices are retained only for statutory accounting periods required under Indian tax law.
  • Payment & Refund Audit Logs: Payment transaction references (UPI/card/netbanking/COD status) are retained for fraud prevention, chargeback resolution, and regulatory compliance.
  • Ongoing Fulfillments: If you have an open shipment, cancellation, or refund request, deletion finalizes once the transaction is settled.
For full details on how we collect, process, protect, and retain data, please read our Assam Crafts Privacy Policy.
